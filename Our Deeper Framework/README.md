# Our Deeper Framework

*The theory and design beneath the practices.*

These documents are the ground the practices rest on — the deeper cosmology, the operating rhythms, and the systemic design of The Experience of We. None of them is required in order to practice. They are here for when you want to understand what the work is finally in service of, and how it is built.

1. [**The Sacred and the Divine**](01%20The%20Sacred%20and%20the%20Divine.md) — the spiritual heart, offered in three voices (sacred, systems, and plain) so you can enter through the one that speaks your language.
2. [**The Rhythm of Persistence and Becoming**](02%20The%20Rhythm%20of%20Persistence%20and%20Becoming.md) — how living systems grow: the rhythm of immanence and transcendence, persistence and return.
3. [**The Relational Ark**](03%20The%20Relational%20Ark.md) — how the journey of two becomes the infrastructure of many: Cells, the Relational Operating System, and Integration-Limited Scaling.
